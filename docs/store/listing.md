## Description

QRSnip works like Snipping Tool, but for QR codes.

Press F10 (or click New scan), drag a box around any QR code on your screen, and QRSnip reads it instantly. Open the link, copy the text, or copy a Wi-Fi password, right next to where you selected.

Perfect for QR codes in slides, PDFs, video calls, websites and chat screenshots, where your phone can't easily reach.

• Snip to scan: a frozen, dimmed view of your desktop just like Snipping Tool
• Finds every QR code on every screen by itself the moment you press F10
• Scan pictures too: open, drag in or paste photos and screenshots
• Or hold a printed code up to your webcam
• Understands links, Wi-Fi, email, phone, SMS, contacts and map locations
• Reads several codes at once, including tiny and light-on-dark codes
• Also reads Data Matrix, Aztec and PDF417
• Global shortcut (F10, configurable) and a tray icon
• Private scan history on your device, with Wi-Fi passwords hidden
• Works offline. Nothing is uploaded, ever. The camera is used only while you scan with it
• Multiple monitors and any display scaling
• Designed for Windows 11: Mica, Fluent icons, light and dark theme

Free and open source: github.com/nakultt/QRSnip
